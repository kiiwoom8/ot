# Privacy Policy for OT Recorder

**Effective Date:** July 17, 2026

OT Recorder is a Chrome browser extension that helps users track overtime records, allowances, and monthly summaries. This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension collects and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

OT Recorder stores data locally on your device using Chrome's local storage. This may include:

- Overtime records you enter
- Settings and profile information such as wage rates and multipliers
- Export/import data that you create or upload yourself

## 2. How We Use Your Information

The extension uses this information only to provide the features of the app, including calculating overtime summaries and displaying your saved records. Your data is not used for advertising, analytics, or marketing purposes.

## 3. Data Storage and Sharing

OT Recorder does not send your data to external servers or third-party services. Your information is stored locally in your browser and remains under your control. We do not collect or share personal data with third parties.

## 4. Permissions

The extension requests the **storage** permission to save your records and settings locally in your browser.

## 5. User Control

You can delete your stored data from the extension's settings interface at any time. You may also clear local browser data through your browser settings.

## 6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the contact information provided in the Chrome Web Store listing for OT Recorder.

By using OT Recorder, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
